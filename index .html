Happy Birthday meri jaan ❤️

Allah kare Apki zindagi mein bohat si aur khushiyon bhari birthdays aayen, meri baby. Ap meri heroine hain, meri khoobsurat princess.

Meri rowdy, meri cutie, meri queen, meri duniya, meri khushi, meri sweetheart, meri special one... Ap Allah ka sab se khoobsurat tohfa hain.

Main Apse bohat zyada mohabbat karta hoon meri jaan. Meri dua hai ke aane wali har birthday hum saath celebrate karein aur main hamesha Apki zindagi ka hissa rahun.

Shukriya mujhe meri har achi aur buri baat ke saath qabool karne ke liye. Hamare darmiyan utar chadhav aaye, lekin humne kabhi ek dusre ka saath nahi chhoda. Ap hamesha mujhe khush mehsoos karwati hain.

Ap meri zindagi ki sab se khoobsurat aur sab se behtareen insaan hain. Main bohat khushnaseeb hoon ke Ap meri life mein hain. Main bohat khush hoon ke Ap meri zindagi mein aayi.

Apka din bohat khoobsurat, khushiyon se bhara aur yaadgaar guzre. Zindagi ke har mod par Apko kamyabi mile, kyun ke Ap waqai is ke haqdar hain.

Hamesha khush rehna. Hamesha mere saath rehna. Ap meri khushi hain. Hamesha muskurati rehna.

Dua hai hum dono mil kar apne bade sapne poore karein aur har manzil saath hasil karein.

Ap jahan bhi rahen, hamesha khush rahen. Allah Apki har dua qabool kare aur Apki zindagi hamesha mohabbat aur khushiyon se bhari rahe. 🤍

Ek baar phir...
